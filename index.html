This page explains how users of BYTEX Calculated Pro can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

Email: naweenshashipriya410@gmail.com

2. Use the subject: Account Deletion Request.

What will be deleted?

When a valid deletion request is processed, we will delete or anonymize personal account information associated with the requested account, subject to any information that we are legally required to retain.

Data retention

Some information may be retained when required by applicable law, security requirements, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other legitimate legal obligations. Any retained information will be kept only for the period required for the applicable purpose.

Important

Deleting your account may permanently remove access to your account and associated data. Account deletion cannot necessarily be reversed.
